{% if data=="Actinic Keratosis - Benign." %}

Medical Treatment:

Cryotherapy, Prescription 5-fluorouracil (5-FU), Prescription imiquimod, Tirbanibulin, Photodynamic therapy, Curettage or other lesion-directed procedures.


Foods to Eat

Green leafy vegetables,Broccoli, carrots, tomatoes and other vegetables,Fruits such as oranges, guava, papaya and berries, Lentils, beans and chickpeas, Eggs, Fish/chicken, Nuts and seeds, Whole grains, Milk/curd if tolerated, Plenty of water


Foods to Avoid

There is no specific food that must be completely avoided because of AK. Avoiding rice, milk, eggs, fish or chicken is not medically required. Limit highly processed foods and sugary drinks as part of a generally healthy diet.

{% elif data=="Basal Cell Carcinoma - Malignant." %}

Medical Treatment:

Surgical excision,Mohs micrographic surgery, Curettage and electrodesiccation, Cryosurgery, Radiation therapy, Photodynamic therapy, Selected topical treatments such as imiquimod, Advanced cases may require targeted/systemic treatment such as vismodegib, sonidegib or other specialist treatments.


Foods to Eat

Vegetables, Fruits, Whole grains, Beans/lentils, Eggs, Fish/chicken, Nuts/seeds, Adequate protein, Plenty of water


Foods to Avoid

There is no BCC-specific food that must be completely avoided. Limit highly processed foods and sugary drinks. Avoid alcohol if a treating doctor advises avoiding it, particularly during cancer treatment.

{% elif data=="Dermatofibroma - Malignant." %}

Medical Treatment:

No treatment is required if the diagnosis is clear and it is not causing problems. A dermatologist may recommend removal or biopsy if the diagnosis is uncertain or the lesion is troublesome.


Foods to Eat

Normal balanced diet, Vegetables, Fruits, Dal/beans, Eggs, Fish/chicken, Whole grains, Nuts/seeds, Water


Foods to Avoid

No specific food restriction.You do not need to stop milk, eggs, rice, fish or chicken because of dermatofibroma.

{% elif data=="Melanoma - Malignant." %}

Medical Treatment:

Surgical removal, Immunotherapy, Targeted therapy, Radiation in selected situations, Other specialist cancer treatments.


Foods to Eat

Vegetables, Whole fruits, Beans/lentils, Whole grains, Eggs, Fish/chicken, Nuts/seeds, Adequate protein, Adequate fluids


Foods to Avoid

Highly processed foods, Sugary drinks, Excessive sweets, Processed meats, Large amounts of red meat, Alcohol, particularly when the medical team advises avoiding it

{% elif data=="Nevus - Benign." %}

Medical Treatment:

A stable benign nevus usually needs no medicine. If it looks suspicious or changes, a dermatologist may examine it and sometimes perform a biopsy or remove it.


Foods to Eat

Normal balanced diet, Vegetables, Fruits, Dal/beans, Eggs, Fish/chicken, Whole grains, Nuts/seeds, Water


Foods to Avoid

No specific food restriction. No need to stop milk, egg, rice, fish or chicken.

{% elif data=="Pigmented Benign Keratosis - Benign." %}

Medical Treatment:

If confidently diagnosed as benign, treatment may not be necessary. If the lesion is irritating or the diagnosis is uncertain, a dermatologist may recommend removal or biopsy.


Foods to Eat

Vegetables, Fruits, Dal/beans, Eggs, Fish/chicken, Whole grains, Nuts/seeds, Water


Foods to Avoid

No specific food needs to be completely avoided. Limit highly processed foods and excess sugary drinks for general health.

{% elif data=="Seborrheic Keratosis - Benign." %}

Foods to Eat

Normal balanced diet, Vegetables, Fruits, Lentils/beans, Eggs, Fish/chicken, Whole grains, Nuts/seeds, Water


Foods to Avoid

No specific food needs to be completely avoided. Limit highly processed foods and excess sugary drinks for general health.

{% elif data=="Vascular Lesion - Benign." %}

Usually no specific food restriction. Any restriction would depend on the exact vascular condition and other medical factors.
